# Leadstate Incorporated

> New York City real estate and design technology company (founded 2021, backed by 500 Global) building
> AI and AR tooling for planning, developing, designing, and marketing spaces. Products: ReelProp
> (property photo and interior sketch to marketing video) and RealMe In (3D asset generation).
> Leadstate publishes no public API, developer portal, SDK, or machine-readable developer surface as of
> 2026-07-19 — this file describes the company's public web surface only.

## Products

- [Photo-to-Video](https://www.leadstate.com/restore): Turns static property photography into listing video reels.
- [Sketch-to-Video](https://www.leadstate.com/sketch): Animates interior design sketches into video.
- [CADgpt](https://www.leadstate.com/cadgpt): Generates 3D models from text descriptions.
- [Image-to-3D](https://www.leadstate.com/image3d): Converts 2D images into 3D assets.
- [FloorPlan-to-3D](https://www.leadstate.com/floorplan): Builds 3D visualizations from floor plans.
